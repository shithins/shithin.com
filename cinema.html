Cinema & Art

Cinema & Artistic Works

Screenwriting, directing, acting, and the pursuit of honest, grounded visual storytelling.

Cinema is where I explore human contradictions, silences, and everyday absurdities. In a world full of noise, film is the one medium where rhythm, subtext, and framing allow us to feel what cannot easily be spoken.

My creative work spans writing original screenplays, directing independent short films, performing as an actor, and writing analytical film criticism.

Directing & Screenwriting

Every strong film starts with a story that knows its own heartbeat. My screenwriting gravitates toward naturalistic dialogues, quiet psychological tension, and stories deeply rooted in the cultural textures of Kerala.

As a director, I believe the camera should be an observant witness rather than an intrusive gimmick. Mise-en-scène, pacing, and actor freedom on set take precedence over flashy transitions.

  • Narrative Realism Crafting dialogue that mirrors how real people speak, pause, and hesitate
    Writing
  • Visual Composition Using space, blocking, and lighting to express what the dialogue leaves unsaid
    Direction
  • Independent Shorts Developing low-budget, high-concept narratives under Ekalavyan Studios
    Production

Acting & Performance

Acting taught me empathy. Stepping into a character’s shoes demands shedding ego and listening intensely to your scene partner.

Whether playing grounded, conflicted protagonists or unpredictable supporting roles, I focus on internal motivation, subtle physical mannerisms, and finding truth in the moment.

Film Criticism & Deconstruction

A great filmmaker is first a voracious, discerning viewer. I regularly dissect cinema—from Malayalam new-wave classics and golden-era gems to global arthouse masterworks.
