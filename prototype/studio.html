The Journey

From Computer Science to Enterprise Search Leadership.

I graduated with a BSc in Computer Science, grounded in programming fundamentals, operating systems, and web architecture. Early on, I realized that writing code alone isn't enough if people cannot find what you build.

That led me deep into Technical SEO — not keyword stuffing or vanity metrics, but the hard engineering layer: crawl budgets, server logs, rendering performance, and structured entity graphs. Today, as SEO Team Lead at Acodez IT Solutions, I guide organic search strategy across global enterprises, outranking multi-billion-dollar SaaS players.

Simultaneously, together with close friends and collaborators, I co-founded theblackit — an independent product lab from Kerala where we incubate sovereign software, Chrome extensions, and community apps.
