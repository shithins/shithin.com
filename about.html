About Me

Building digital systems by day, telling visual stories by night.

I’ve always lived between two worlds: the analytical discipline of code and the emotional instincts of cinema.

To me, they aren’t opposites. In SEO and software development, you build systems that respond precisely to what humans search for. In screenwriting and directing, you craft stories that reflect what humans feel. Both crafts require observing human behavior, cutting out unnecessary noise, and delivering truth.

The Path So Far

My foundation began with a BSc in Computer Science at the College of Applied Science, Thamarassery. Rather than choosing between pure software development and digital marketing, I brought engineering rigor into the world of search algorithms.

Over the past 4 years, I worked through high-stakes search environments at CodeAce IT Solutions—quickly moving from Analyst into an elite 5-member core algorithm unit, tackling competitive global verticals and breathing new life into expired domain architectures.

Today, I serve as SEO Team Lead at Acodez IT Solutions in Calicut, managing operations, mentoring rising talent at AVIV Digital Institute, and helping major brands outrank enterprise domains through Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), Core Web Vitals, and technical precision.

Whenever I encounter repetitive agency friction, I build tools to fix it. That drive gave birth to TheBlackit, as well as published Chrome extensions like Keeptime, Clockroach, and Brain Crawl.

The Artistic Lens

Outside the terminal, cinema is my greatest obsession. I act, write screenplays, direct independent short films, and write deep-dive film criticism.

Along with close collaborators, I founded Ekalavyan Studios, an independent creative studio and YouTube channel where we produce original short films and visual stories. Whether in front of the camera or behind the monitor calling cuts, filmmaking keeps me grounded in empathy and human connection.
