Questions I get asked

The objections that come up most often before an engagement starts.
Can you work with our existing development team?

That's the usual setup. I produce tickets your engineers can act on, review implementations and verify what shipped. If they're comfortable with me contributing code directly, that's usually faster.

Do you do content writing as well?

I do content strategy, briefs and structural editing, plus technical writing where the topic is search. For long-form editorial at volume I'll brief and review rather than write everything myself.

How long before we see something?

Technical wins often show within weeks: indexation recoveries, crawl budget, page speed. Competitive ranking and entity gains compound over three to six months. I'll tell you which kind of result to expect at each stage.

What about AI search and GEO?

It runs alongside classic SEO, not instead of it. Entities, schema, structure and citable content are the same foundation that makes answer engines comfortable quoting you.

Do you sign NDAs and work with agencies?

Yes to both. A good share of my work is specialist support behind agency teams, including white-label technical audits.
