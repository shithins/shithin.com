02 — Process Five steps, every time
i. Measure Crawl, logs, field data, Search Console, analytics and the money pages. We agree the baseline before anyone touches the site. You get a baseline report and a definition of winning, in numbers.
ii. Diagnose Separate what moves revenue from what merely looks broken. Most sites have three real problems, not eighty. You get root-cause findings with evidence, ranked by impact and effort.
iii. Build Dev-ready tickets, code where it is mine to write, and verification that each fix actually shipped to production. You get tickets, implementations, QA and crawl verification.
iv. Compound Once the foundation holds: entity work, GEO, content structure and internal linking to raise the ceiling. You get a content and GEO programme aligned to query intent.
v. Report Plain-language reporting on the numbers that matter: qualified organic sessions, rankings that convert, AI citations. You get a monthly readout with next month's bets.
03 — FAQ Questions I get asked

The things worth asking before you hire anyone.

Can you work with our existing development team?

That is the usual setup. I produce tickets your engineers can act on, review implementations and verify what shipped. If they are comfortable with me contributing code directly, that is usually faster.

Do you do content writing as well?

I do content strategy, briefs and structural editing, plus technical writing where the topic is search. For long-form editorial at volume I will brief and review rather than write everything myself.

How long before we see something?

Technical wins often show within weeks: indexation recoveries, crawl budget, page speed. Competitive ranking and entity gains compound over three to six months. I will tell you which kind of result to expect at each stage.

What about AI search and GEO?

It runs alongside classic SEO, not instead of it. Entities, schema, structure and citable content are the same foundation that makes answer engines comfortable quoting you.

Do you sign NDAs and work with agencies?

Yes to both. A good share of my work is specialist support behind agency teams, including white-label technical audits.
